## Key Learnings

* Learned what Prompt Engineering is and why it is important when working with AI.
* Learned that a clear, detailed, and structured prompt can produce better AI responses.
* Understood the difference between a simple (Lazy) Prompt and an Engineered Prompt.
* Learned how adding context, instructions, requirements, and desired output format can improve the quality of an AI response.
* Compared the outputs generated from both prompts and observed differences in explanation quality, structure, examples, and image design.
* Learned that better prompts help AI understand the task more accurately and generate more useful and relevant results.
* Explored MetaPrompt, a tool that helps improve and structure prompts before using them with AI.
* Practiced writing and refining prompts to communicate more effectively with AI.
